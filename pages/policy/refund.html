EzyPayment – Refunds, Failed Payments & Chargebacks

Last Updated: 30 November 2025

EzyPayment acts only as a technology platform and payment facilitator. We do not sell any product or service directly. All refunds are processed strictly as per the merchant’s refund policy and applicable payment regulations.

1. Refund Eligibility

  • Refunds are initiated only when the merchant approves the request.
  • Refunds may also be processed for:
    • Failed transactions
    • Duplicate transactions
    • Amount debited but not credited to the beneficiary

2. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Incorrect or invalid payment details entered by the user.
  • Disputes related to the quality of product/service (handled by merchant only).
  • Transactions reported as fraudulent without valid proof.
  • Successful & completed transactions are not eligible for a refund.

3. Refund Processing Time

  • UPI: 2–7 business days
  • Bank Transfer (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS): 3–10 business days
  • Debit/Credit Cards: 7–15 business days

For delays beyond the standard timeline, the issuing bank may require additional time to complete settlement.

4. How to Request a Refund

To raise a refund request, email the details below to:

Email: chargeback@ezypayment.in

  • Transaction ID
  • Date & time of payment
  • Amount paid
  • Payment method (UPI/Card/Bank Transfer)
  • Reason for refund

Your request will be reviewed and forwarded to the respective merchant for approval.
