PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: 30 November 2025

EzyPayments (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your personal, financial, and transactional data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and safeguard your information when you access our website, products, or payment services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number.
  • Payment & Transaction Details: Transaction IDs, payment mode, amount, UPI/bank/card data (processed securely).
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser details, device information, access logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process payments and transactions
  • Verify customers and merchants
  • Prevent fraud and enhance security
  • Provide support & improve user experience
  • Comply with regulatory and legal requirements

We do not sell or trade your personal data.

3. Security & Compliance

  • PCI DSS compliant payment processing
  • ISO-aligned data security standards
  • Regular vulnerability & penetration testing (VA/PT)
  • Encrypted storage and secure APIs
  • RBI-compliant data localization (data stored in India)

4. Data Sharing

We share data only with trusted entities:

  • Banks, UPI/card networks, and payment gateway partners
  • Technology and infrastructure service providers
  • Government or legal authorities when required

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as required for regulatory, security, operational, and accounting purposes. Unnecessary data is securely deleted.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Request corrections or updates
  • Delete non-essential information
  • Withdraw consent
  • Opt-out of promotional messages

7. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from individuals below 18 years of age. In case of accidental collection, we promptly delete such information.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
